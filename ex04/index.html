Comparing content-box and border-box sizing.

Box Sizing Comparison

Content Box

content-box

Width: 200px

Padding: 20px

Border: 5px

Total width: 250px

Border Box

border-box

Width: 200px

Padding: 20px

Border: 5px

Total width: 200px

Understanding the Difference

Content-box adds padding and borders outside the declared width.

Border-box includes padding and borders inside the declared width.
