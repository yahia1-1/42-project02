Opacity vs RGBA

Opacity: 0.5

This text also becomes transparent.

RGBA: 0.5

This text stays fully visible.

Explanation

Opacity affects the entire element, including its text and child elements.

RGBA makes only the background color transparent, while the text remains fully visible.
